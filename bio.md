## Long (215 words) — board nominations, keynote programs, detailed profiles

Michelle Chang is a design founder and systems-thinker based in San Francisco,
where she was born and raised. She holds a degree in Environmental Sciences from
UC Berkeley.

Over eight years she has designed decision tools for experts in privacy and
security, agriculture, maritime shipping, climate tech, construction, and
applied AI. The through line is legibility: making systems too large to see at
once — a shipping route, a carbon inventory, a field across a growing season —
understandable to the person who has to act on them today.

At Corteva Agrisciences she designed the data entry experience behind a carbon
credit program that enrolled 1.1 million acres across 31 states, reaching a 94%
submission rate and cutting customer support time by 60%. At Sofar Ocean she
built the layer that let shoreside operators understand why captains deviate
from routing guidance, rather than simply penalizing them for it. At Kojo
Technologies she led a workflow redesign that removed hours of manual entry from
construction quoting. As design lead for PERSUIT's AI agent Persi, she developed
the principles, content design, and design system components behind a beta that
became a deciding factor in customer renewals.

She now runs Seafoam Studio, her independent practice spanning freelance design,
dance immersives, and community crafts and arts programming, and hosts the Sea
People podcast, a space to share stories of the sea and the people who steward
it. She published a note in the Marine Pollution Bulletin on microplastics in
wastewater streams, and volunteers at the California Academy of Sciences,
leading informal science education with school-age children and families on
coral reef ecosystems.

### Points to make in a cover letter, not the bio

The bio establishes credibility. These are the arguments that address what the
posting actually asks for:

- **You already do ocean storytelling.** The Sea People podcast is the single
  closest thing to what IOFF exists to do — surfacing stories of the sea and its
  stewards. Their Youth Ocean Storytelling Challenge is the same work aimed at
  students. Say how many episodes, who you've interviewed, and what you've
  learned about drawing a story out of someone.
- **Running programs is your current job.** The studio's community arts
  programming and dance immersives are the closest thing on your résumé to
  "coordinate student participation and support the Youth Ocean Storytelling
  Challenge." Lead with it. Give a concrete example: how many people, how often,
  how you found them.
- **Educator relationships.** Cal Academy is direct evidence of engaging both
  children and the adults with them — closer to the job than most design work.
- **Youth programming.** Teaching children's dance is real instructional
  experience with young people. Say the age range and how long.
- **Storytelling.** The Youth Ocean Storytelling Challenge is about helping kids
  communicate ocean science. Publishing marine research and doing informal
  science education is that skill in two registers.
- **Coordination and facilitation.** You ran workshops with 11–13 cross-
  functional participants at PERSUIT and built journey maps with product,
  engineering, and customer success. That's the "organized and proactive"
  requirement, evidenced.
- **Bay Area and capacity.** You're local for the occasional in-person work.
  Running your own studio means you control your schedule — say plainly that
  3–5 hours a week fits.

## Notes on using these

**Fill in before sending.** Consider adding, where relevant:

- Named employers in the short and medium versions — currently only in the long
  one, since some contexts prefer the work over the logos
- Years or age range for the dance teaching and Cal Academy volunteering;
  duration signals commitment
- The Marine Pollution Bulletin citation in full, for academic or science
  contexts
- Pronouns, if you include them

**Tailor the emphasis.** For a climate or conservation board, lead with Corteva
and Carbon Direct. For a technology or civic board, lead with the privacy
non-profit and Persi. For arts, education, or community organizations, lead with
the studio and Cal Academy. The medium version is the easiest to reorder.

**Naming the non-profit** would strengthen every version it appears in. "A
privacy and security non-profit" is vaguer than it needs to be if the
organization is one people would recognize.
