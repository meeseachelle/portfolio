## Context

Persi is PERSUIT's legal operations agent. It helps Fortune 500 legal teams
decide when to keep a matter in house versus hire outside counsel, weighing
subject matter, size, geography, and cost.

I joined as design lead during experimentation, while the model and data
architecture were still being worked out. Persi had to sit inside a platform
users already knew: enough cohesion to feel like PERSUIT, enough distinction to
signal a genuinely new kind of experience.

## The key decision

**Where the agent hands off to a human.** An agent that never hands off
frustrates people exactly when stakes are highest. One that hands off too readily
never earns trust. Setting that line shaped everything downstream.

## What I did

**Grounded the team in evidence before setting principles.** I ran competitive
analysis across adjacent software and leading AI chat products, then shared it
out. Without shared reference points, principle-setting becomes taste, and taste
doesn't scale.

## De-risking through content design

When an agent hands a general counsel a benchmark figure, the words around that
number decide whether it gets acted on, ignored, or escalated. Content design is
the trust surface.

## Outcome

- **22 customers** on the Persi beta program
- A **deciding factor in renewing their contracts** with PERSUIT

Research sessions with a subset showed they wanted benchmarking data faster,
and a partner in the in-house versus outside-counsel decision, not a system that
made it for them.

That validated the intervention line. The value wasn't automating judgment. It
was removing the work getting in the way of it.
