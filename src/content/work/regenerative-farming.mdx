## Context

The Corteva Carbon Initiative generates registry-issued carbon credits for
farmers at scale. Farmers enter their practice change data into Granular
Insights, Corteva's free tool for measuring impact and earning additional
income.

Qualifying takes an enormous amount of data entry, on top of actually farming.

## The key decision

Most participants adopt practice changes for agronomic reasons and treat carbon
payment as a bonus. So the product couldn't use the payout as the reward for
finishing a long form. The reward had to be **legible progress** and confidence
the data was right.

## What I did

**Mapped the work with the people fielding the complaints.** Journey mapping
with customer success surfaced the questions the product should have been
answering itself.

**Tested three information hierarchies with 12 farmers.** Farmers organize
records in strikingly different ways, so I sketched all three plausible
orderings rather than polishing one. Cheap sketches are easier to disagree with
than finished screens.

**Cut the form down conditionally**, so farmers who skipped a practice never see
its questions.

  src={entryForm}

**Added bulk actions** after feedback that manual entry got repetitive. The edge
cases carried the work: what happens when some fields are already filled, and
when none are left.

## Outcome

- **1.1M** acres enrolled across **31** states
- **94%** submission rate
- **60%** reduction in customer success time

The 60% is the one I point to: product guidance replaced a human explaining the
same thing over and over.
